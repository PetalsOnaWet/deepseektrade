Crypto AI Trading Live

Crypto AI Trading Live 团队 runs a live Binance Futures sub-account with a current balance of 20 USDT. The trading engine is fully autonomous, powered by the DeepSeek reasoning model. Every three minutes the AI evaluates market data, risk metrics, and open positions, then pushes structured decisions into the execution pipeline. Trades, logs, and performance are streamed to this dashboard in real time.

What the system does

  • Connects directly to Binance USDT-margined perpetual contracts.
  • Streams live account equity, margin usage, and open positions.
  • Visualises AI reasoning, CoT logs, and every execution record.
  • Publishes canonical share cards via ShareThis for social updates.

Is it real trading?

Yes. Orders are sent with a valid futures API key stored in the server config. Position data, realised P&L, and balance changes are fetched from Binance on every cycle. There is no paper trading or replayed market feed.

Which model is driving the trades?

The decision loop calls the DeepSeek reasoning model through our model control plane. Its outputs are validated locally: leverage is capped at 10×, risk per position must stay below 3 % of equity, and JSON responses must include a complete protection plan.

Core strategy

  • Trend-following bias — positions are filtered by 3 minute, 4 hour, daily, and weekly structure.
  • Two-stage risk control — once price moves 3 % in favour, the AI raises the stop to breakeven with a small buffer.
  • Trailing protection — after a further 1.5–2 % move, a volatility-aware trailing stop (0.8–1.5 %) keeps profits locked while allowing continuation.
  • Position rationing — at most three simultaneous symbols with risk capped at 3 % of equity per trade.
